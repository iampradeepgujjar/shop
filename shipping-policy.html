Logistics & Dispatch

Pan-India Delivery & Packaging Standards

📅 Last Updated: September 2026 • Tracked Express Couriers
Free Express Shipping: Orders with a cart value of ₹2,999 and above qualify for 100% Free Shipping throughout India. A flat shipping charge of ₹149 applies to orders under ₹2,999.

1. Processing & Dispatch Timelines

Orders placed on DishaMail are processed rapidly from our central hub in Noorpur, Uttar Pradesh:

  • Standard Orders: Dispatched within 24 business hours (Monday to Saturday, excluding national holidays).
  • Custom PC Assemblies: Dispatched within 48 to 72 business hours to accommodate component stress testing, cable management, and BIOS benchmarking.

3. Secure Hardware Packaging Standards

Delicate computer hardware requires specialized handling. DishaMail packages every shipment according to strict electrical engineering standards:

  • Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) Protection: Components remain inside factory antistatic bags.
  • Multi-Layer Cushioning: High-density air bubble wrap and shock-absorbent corner protectors.
  • Tamper-Proof Outer Boxes: Reinforced 5-ply corrugated cardboard sealed with DishaMail branded security tape.

4. Shipment Tracking & Courier Partners

We partner with premier Indian logistics providers, including BlueDart, Delhivery, DTDC, Xpressbees, and India Post. As soon as your order is handed to the carrier, a real-time Tracking ID and dispatch link are generated and sent to your registered email and phone number.

5. In-Transit Damage & Inspection Protocol

Delivery Precaution: Please inspect the exterior parcel condition before accepting delivery. If the outer carton is noticeably torn, crushed, or resealed with unbranded tape, note "Package Received Damaged" on the delivery receipt and contact us immediately within 48 hours at contact@dishamail.site.
