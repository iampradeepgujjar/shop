Our Story

From Passion to India's Premier Tech Store

DishaMail was founded in Noorpur, Distt. Bijnor, Uttar Pradesh by a team of hardware engineers and computer enthusiasts who were frustrated with the lack of genuine, affordable computer components and trusted repair facilities. We set out to bridge the gap with transparent pricing, official manufacturer warranties, and lightning-fast customer support.

Today, DishaMail offers genuine products from premier global brands including Intel, AMD, NVIDIA, Corsair, ASUS, and Samsung — coupled with professional computer repair services, doorstep support, and reliable pan-India courier shipping.
