Our Story

From Passion to India's Premier Tech Store

TechNexus was founded in 2015 in Bangalore by a team of hardware enthusiasts who were frustrated with the lack of genuine, affordable computer components in India. We started in a small 200 sq.ft shop and have since grown to serve over 50,000 customers nationwide.

Today, we offer 5,000+ genuine products from top brands including Intel, AMD, NVIDIA, Corsair, and more — with professional repair services, a dedicated support team, and pan-India shipping.
